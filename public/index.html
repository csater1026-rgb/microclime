Your yard has its own weather.

Weather apps report your whole zip code. Microclime maps the sun, shade, and frost risk for one exact spot — using a photo of your real horizon, not a generic average.

2. Trace your real horizon

Take a photo from the exact spot, holding your phone level and pointed at one direction. Then trace the skyline — where trees, roofs, or fences block the sky. Click along the photo to mark the blocking height at each point.

In degrees, like a compass: 0 = North, 90 = East, 180 = South, 270 = West. Open the Compass app on your phone, face the same way you took the photo, and type the number it shows.
Leave this at 60 unless you know your phone's camera is different — it's right for most phones.

Trace your horizon as it really looks first. Then hit this button, and try editing the trace to test an idea — like adding a bump where a new tree would grow, or flattening a spot where you'd trim one back — to see what it would actually change.
